## Immaculate Conception Catholic Church — Roslyn

### 1886
The history of Catholicism began with the town of Roslyn itself in 1886. Masses were celebrated by a priest from Ellensburg in the Christopher Meneghel home in Roslyn and the Boyle Mulvaney residence in Ronald.

### 1887
The first baptism was Jon Covine, son of John and Celestine Colvine, on June 26, 1887 by Fr. Aloysius Parodi, S.J. The first marriage was validated on January 9, 1887. The graceful high-towered church was erected on a hillside overlooking the town by Father L. Kusters in 1887. The first recorded marriage united H. Lupak and Amelia Ralkowski on February 8, 1891. Fr. J. Sweens served the parish from October 1897 to November 1900. The first recorded First Communion class was August 1898. Bishop O'Dea celebrated the first Confirmation ceremony in July 1900.

### 1900–1926
Fr. P.E. Kornke came to Roslyn in 1900, but also assisted in Ellensburg. The first rectory, a small two-room house, was east of the church. Fr. Kornke built the present rectory north of the church in 1905. Fr. Demetrius enlarged it. Fr. Kornke left in September 1906. Fr. Stenicus Bontampo was a resident priest for the month of December. He was followed by Frs. Aloysius Milner (May 1907–1908), Mehelich, F.T. Simonek, Fred Marineck, and J. Sweems. Toward the end of 1908, Fr. P.E. Kornke returned and remained until October 1916. Fr. Augustine Osgniach, O.S.B. came as the pastor in November 1916 until May 1919. He was followed by a number of Benedictines, some for only a Sunday, others longer. Some were able to read the Gospel and preach in Croatian. These included Frs. Neary, Lawrence Pietrzkowski, and Alfred Gendreau. In July 1921, Fr. Anthony Hack arrived and remained until August 1926. The first bazaar was held to raise money for lowering the church ceiling, fixing the foundation, and putting in furnaces for both church and rectory, replacing the old wood stoves. Fr. Theodore Wolters stayed from October 1926 to August and purchased the crucifix on the tabernacle.

### 1927–1937
Fr. Dominick Hack followed from November 1927 to June 1929. Fr. Demetrius Junaman followed from 1929 to 1931. Fr. Clement Regimbal, O.S.B. arrived in November 1931. In September 1932, fire damaged the church and it was rebuilt. The church bell was cracked in the fire and the new bell is from the old South School in Roslyn. Fr. Regimbal passed away December 1, 1933 at the age of 42. Fr. Francis O. Driscoll stayed from January to July 1934. Fr. Paul Kucera, O.S.B. arrived in July 1934 and stayed until August 1937. He was a carpenter always repairing and working on the grounds. He remodeled the kitchen and added a bedroom to the rectory.

### 1937–1955
Fr. Fabian Sexton took over in September 1937 and stayed until July 1947. He built the parish hall in 1942 and restored a back room in the church for a winter chapel. He also started a fund for a new organ. Fr. Vincent Carey (October 1947 to September 1948) saw the new concrete steps to the church built. In 1948, Fr. Damian Glenn helped five Sundays and Fr. Paul Kucera returned in November 1948 and stayed until October 1952. Fr. Ed Weckert, Pastor of St. John's in Cle Elum, became Administrator. He was helped by various Benedictines from time to time. Fr. Fred O'Hearne, Pastor in Ellensburg, followed as Administrator, being helped by several Diocesan priests who were in special ministries.

### 1955–1966
Msgr. Joseph Sondergeld arrived with his sister, Bertha, on January 7, 1955. The church and rectory were painted inside and out. New floor covering, contact paper on the windows, new pews, and new roof were added to the church. He left in September 1961. Fr. Patrick Byron was pastor then and started the CCID program. He left because of ill health. Fr. Sondergeld returned on January 13, 1962. He celebrated his fiftieth anniversary of priesthood on September 8, 1966. Ill health forced him to retire in 1967 and he died on November 16, 1969.

### 1967–1975
Fr. Harvey Owen came in 1967 and made many improvements. The rectory and winter chapel were remodeled, a Reconciliation chapel replaced the confessionals, and an altar of sacrifice was purchased for Mass facing the people. The first Eucharistic Ministers, Donovan Hill and Lester Ruben Beedle, were commissioned. A farewell potluck was held April 28, 1975. Fr. Brian Gallagher became pastor and when he left, Fr. William Byron, Pastor of St. John's Cle Elum, assumed responsibility for both parishes.

### 1975–1999
Fr. Joseph Graaff became pastor in June 1979. He renovated the Winter Chapel with paneled walls, a rug, pews, electric heat, and an Altar of Sacrifice. He was pastor through 1988. Fr. John Marasciulo became Pastor of both parishes in Cle Elum and Roslyn. Stained glass windows were added to the church at this time. In May 1994, Fr. William Byron became Pastor and served the parish until January 9, 1999.

### 1999–2000
Fr. Ronald J. Patnode was named Pastor of St. John the Baptist Church and Immaculate Conception Church in January 1999. As he went about the two parishes administering pastoral care, his frequent companion was his little rescued dog, Daisy. Fr. Patnode was responsible for transferring office records to a computerized system which connected the church office to the diocese database. He was appointed Pastor of St. Joseph Parish in Sunnyside in January 2000. He passed on to our Lord on November 24, 2013.

### 2000–2012
Fr. Lawrence T. Reilly followed Fr. Patnode as Pastor of the two Upper County Churches in June 2001. During his time, a beautiful renovation of the exterior of St. John the Baptist Church was accomplished. Immaculate Conception Church was also beautifully renovated inside and received structural reinforcement following an earthquake in 2001 which damaged the steeple. Fr. Reilly was responsible for funding the Holy Spirit Library. Bishop Carlos A. Sevilla, S.J. retired, becoming Bishop Emeritus. In May 2008 and May 23, 2010, Bishop Carlos Sevilla confirmed a combined class from Immaculate Conception and St. John the Baptist at Immaculate Conception. The Yakima Diocese welcomed its new shepherd, Bishop Joseph J. Tyson, on May 31, 2011. On August 8, 2011, Bishop Joseph Tyson celebrated Mass at Immaculate Conception for the 125th Anniversary.

### 2012–2015
Upon the retirement of Fr. Reilly in June 2012, Fr. Brooks F. Beaulaurier became the Pastor of the Catholic Communities of the Upper County Churches on July 29, 2012. In the time since his installation as Pastor, the two churches have seen a return of religious education of children from preschool through high school, the formation of a Knights of Columbus Council, and the addition of a statue of St. John the Baptist to the front of the church of that name in celebration of the 100th Anniversary.

### 2016–2019
July 2018, Fr. Brooks Beaulaurier is reassigned. The Most Rev. Joseph J. Tyson, Bishop of Yakima, installs the Very Rev. Father Francisco Higuera as Pastor of the Catholic Communities of Immaculate Conception, Roslyn, WA and St. John the Baptist, Cle Elum, WA. Father Francisco Higuera celebrates his first Masses on July 21 and July 22.

## St. John the Baptist Catholic Church — Cle Elum

### 1880–1910
Aloysius Parodi, S.J., an Italian Jesuit, came to the Kittitas Valley to convert Indians. He became the prime missionary for Kittitas and celebrated the humble beginnings of Catholicism in the Upper Kittitas County. Fr. Parodi was replaced by Fr. L. Custers, Pastor of St. Andrew's Parish in Ellensburg, and the region's four-decade missionary period came to an end. St. John the Baptist Church became a part of the Diocese of Nisqually. Under the supervision of Msgr. Sweens, a subscription was undertaken to build a church. Local citizen Walter Reed donated property for a Catholic Church. Construction began on the corner of 2nd Street and Bullitt. Until this time, services were held in the homes of parishioners. In 1908, the Nisqually Diocese was divided and St. John the Baptist became a part of the Diocese of Seattle.

### 1911–1920
In 1911, Bishop Edward O'Dea, Bishop to the Diocese of Seattle, appointed Fr. Anton Gradjanich as the first Pastor of St. John the Baptist Parish. In the same year, Fr. Augustine Osginiach, O.S.B. was appointed Pastor and served until September 1914. In 1914, an addition to the sacristy and a lot adjoining with a house, which became a parish hall, were completed. In 1915, a series of Redemptorist and Jesuit priests from Seattle served the parish until June 15, when Fr. Alfred Gendreau, O.S.B. from St. Martin's Abbey took over. On June 25, 1918, disaster struck when fire destroyed 29 blocks of downtown Cle Elum, including the church and rectory at 310 East 2nd Street. The Public School Board allowed the church to use school buildings for the celebration of Mass. By November, a new location, 301 and 303 West 2nd Street, was purchased. In 1921, a new brick and veneer church and rectory were built.

### 1933–1962
In 1933, Fr. Edward Weckert, O.S.B., known as "Father Ed," was named as pastor. In 1951, the Diocese of Yakima was created with the Most Rev. Joseph P. Dougherty as the first Bishop. St. John the Baptist became part of the Diocese of Yakima.

### 1963–1967
Fr. Edward Weckert retired to St. Martin's Abbey because of ill health. Father John Shaw was appointed pastor. Renovations were begun to update the church interior according to the norms of the Second Vatican Council. In 1967, Father Patrick Burke was appointed pastor. Renovations continued under Fr. Burke with the addition of stained glass windows. In 1968, Bishop Dougherty retired and was replaced by Bishop Cornelius Power. Bishop Power was subsequently appointed Archbishop of Portland. Bishop Nicolas Walsh was appointed as his successor to shepherd the Diocese of Yakima.

### 1974–1984
Fr. William Byron was appointed pastor. A fire during a mission preached by Fr. San Pietro damaged the sanctuary and a new sacristy and reconciliation room were added. Bishop William S. Skylstad was ordained and installed on May 12 as the fifth bishop of the Diocese of Yakima.

### 1985–1990
Fr. John Marasciulo became pastor of St. John the Baptist on October 13 and was appointed pastor of both St. John the Baptist and Immaculate Conception parishes.

### 1991–1994
Pope John Paul II named Fr. John Marasciulo a Monsignor (Chaplain to His Holiness).

### 1995–1998
Monsignor John Marasciulo "Father John" celebrated his Golden Jubilee (fifty years of priesthood) on July 15. The bishop later named Fr. William Byron pastor of Immaculate Conception. Bishop Carlos Sevilla, S.J. was appointed Bishop of Yakima on February 17, 1997. He named Fr. Ron Patnode pastor to follow the beloved Fr. John Marasciulo. Pat Jacobs was consecrated as a Virgin at St. John the Baptist Church on December 6, 1998. Monsignor John Marasciulo retired on January 9, 1999. Fr. Ronald J. Patnode was officially named Pastor of St. John the Baptist Church and Immaculate Conception Church in Roslyn.

### 2000–2010
Fr. Lawrence T. Reilly was appointed as Pastor of the two Upper County Churches. During his time, the renovation of the exterior of St. John the Baptist Church was completed. Immaculate Conception Church was also renovated inside and received structural reinforcement following an earthquake.

### 2011–2012
Bishop Carlos A. Sevilla, S.J. retired, becoming Bishop Emeritus. The Yakima Diocese welcomed its new shepherd, Bishop Joseph J. Tyson, on May 31. On May 12, 2012, Bishop Joseph Tyson confirmed a combined class from Immaculate Conception and St. John the Baptist at St. John the Baptist. In July, Fr. Reilly retired. The newly appointed Bishop of the Diocese of Yakima, Joseph J. Tyson, installed Fr. Brooks F. Beaulaurier as Pastor of the Catholic Communities of Upper Kittitas County on July 19, 2012.

### 2013–2015
With his assignment, Fr. Beaulaurier, with the assistance of parishioners, initiated religious education for kindergarten through fifth grade children as well as for middle school and high school students. He also introduced continuing faith formation classes in the Catholic faith for adults. He oversaw the formation of Knights of Columbus Council #15684, jointly sponsored by the parishes. Fr. Beaulaurier also oversaw the addition of a statue of St. John the Baptist to the front of the church in celebration of the 100th Anniversary (1914–2014) on May 25, 2014.

### 2016–2019
July 2018, Fr. Beaulaurier was reassigned. The Most Rev. Joseph J. Tyson, Bishop of Yakima, installed the Very Rev. Father Francisco Higuera as Pastor of the Catholic Communities of Immaculate Conception, Roslyn, WA and St. John the Baptist, Cle Elum, WA. Father Francisco Higuera celebrated his first Masses on July 21 and July 22.
