## Immaculate Conception Catholic Church — Roslyn

### 1886
The history of Catholicism began with the town of Roslyn itself in 1886. Masses were celebrated by a priest from Ellensburg in the Christopher Meneghel home in Roslyn and the Boyle Mulvaney residence in Ronald.

### 1887
The first baptism was Jon Covine, son of John and Celestine Colvine, on June 26, 1887 by Fr. Aloysius Parodi, S.J. The first marriage was validated on January 9, 1887. The graceful high-towered church was erected on a hillside overlooking the town by Father L. Kusters in 1887. The first recorded marriage united H. Lupak and Amelia Ralkowski on February 8, 1891. The first recorded First Communion class was August 1898. Bishop O'Dea celebrated the first Confirmation ceremony in July 1900.

### 1900–1926
The parish's early growth outpaced its resources — the original two-room rectory was replaced with a new one in 1905, then later enlarged. A revolving series of priests, including several Benedictines who could read the Gospel and preach in Croatian for the parish's immigrant families, kept the young mission going through the 1910s. The first bazaar, held in 1926, raised money to lower the church ceiling, fix the foundation, and add furnaces to replace the old wood stoves.

### 1927–1937
In September 1932, fire damaged the church; it was rebuilt, and the church bell, cracked in the blaze, was replaced with one recast from Roslyn's old South School. Fr. Clement Regimbal, O.S.B., who arrived in 1931, died in office in December 1933 at just 42. Fr. Paul Kucera, O.S.B., a skilled carpenter, spent his years at the parish (1934–1937, and later 1948–1952) repairing and improving the rectory.

### 1937–1955
Fr. Fabian Sexton (1937–1947) built the parish hall in 1942 and restored a back room in the church as a winter chapel. The postwar years brought new concrete steps to the church, and a string of short-term administrators shared responsibilities with St. John the Baptist in Cle Elum.

### 1955–1966
Msgr. Joseph Sondergeld arrived in 1955 and gave the church and rectory a full refresh — new paint, floors, pews, and roof. He celebrated his 50th anniversary of priesthood in 1966, the year before ill health forced his retirement; he died in 1969.

### 1967–1975
Fr. Harvey Owen (1967–1975) modernized worship at Immaculate Conception: a Reconciliation chapel replaced the confessionals, an altar of sacrifice was added for Mass facing the people, and the parish commissioned its first Eucharistic Ministers, Donovan Hill and Lester Ruben Beedle.

### 1975–1999
Fr. Joseph Graaff (pastor 1979–1988) renovated the Winter Chapel with new paneling, pews, and heat. Fr. John Marasciulo, who became pastor of both Cle Elum and Roslyn afterward, added stained glass windows to the church.

### 1999–2000
Fr. Ronald J. Patnode (1999–2000) modernized the parish office, connecting it to the diocese's new computer database. He was known for his ever-present companion, a rescued dog named Daisy.

### 2000–2012
A major renovation restored St. John the Baptist's exterior and reinforced Immaculate Conception's structure after a 2001 earthquake damaged the steeple. The parish also funded the Holy Spirit Library. Bishop Carlos Sevilla confirmed a combined class from both parishes in 2008 and 2010, and celebrated Immaculate Conception's 125th anniversary Mass in 2011.

### 2012–2015
The two churches saw a return of religious education from preschool through high school, the formation of a Knights of Columbus council, and a new statue of St. John the Baptist marking the church's 100th anniversary.

### 2016–2019
The Most Rev. Joseph J. Tyson, Bishop of Yakima, installed the Very Rev. Francisco Higuera as pastor of both parishes in July 2018; he celebrated his first Masses on July 21 and 22.

## St. John the Baptist Catholic Church — Cle Elum

### 1880–1910
Aloysius Parodi, S.J., an Italian Jesuit missionary, brought the earliest Catholic presence to the Kittitas Valley. Under the Diocese of Nisqually, a subscription campaign and a land donation from local citizen Walter Reed funded the parish's first church, built at the corner of 2nd Street and Bullitt — before this, Mass was said in parishioners' homes. In 1908, the growing diocese was divided and St. John the Baptist became part of the new Diocese of Seattle.

### 1911–1920
In 1911, Bishop Edward O'Dea appointed Fr. Anton Gradjanich as the parish's first official pastor. A sacristy addition and a new parish hall followed in 1914. On June 25, 1918, a fire destroyed 29 blocks of downtown Cle Elum, including the church and rectory; the parish worshipped in the public schools until a new brick and veneer church and rectory were built in 1921 at a new location on West 2nd Street.

### 1933–1962
Fr. Edward Weckert, O.S.B. — known to parishioners as "Father Ed" — served as pastor for three decades starting in 1933. In 1951, the newly created Diocese of Yakima brought St. John the Baptist under its wing.

### 1963–1967
Renovations updated the church interior to reflect the Second Vatican Council, including new stained glass windows added under Fr. Patrick Burke, appointed pastor in 1967.

### 1974–1984
A fire during a parish mission damaged the sanctuary, leading to a new sacristy and reconciliation room. Bishop William S. Skylstad was ordained and installed as the diocese's fifth bishop in 1984.

### 1985–1990
Fr. John Marasciulo became pastor of St. John the Baptist in 1985, and soon after took on Immaculate Conception as well, uniting the two parishes under one pastor for good.

### 1991–1994
Pope John Paul II named Fr. Marasciulo a Monsignor in recognition of his service.

### 1995–1998
Msgr. Marasciulo celebrated his Golden Jubilee — fifty years of priesthood — in 1995. Fr. Ron Patnode was named his successor in 1997, and Pat Jacobs was consecrated as a Virgin at the church in 1998.

### 2000–2010
Fr. Lawrence T. Reilly oversaw an exterior renovation of St. John the Baptist and a structural reinforcement of Immaculate Conception following an earthquake.

### 2011–2012
Bishop Joseph J. Tyson arrived as the diocese's new shepherd in 2011 and confirmed a combined class from both parishes the following year. Upon Fr. Reilly's retirement, Bishop Tyson installed Fr. Brooks F. Beaulaurier as pastor of both Upper Kittitas County parishes in July 2012.

### 2013–2015
Fr. Beaulaurier introduced religious education for children and teens, adult faith formation classes, and a Knights of Columbus council jointly sponsored by both parishes. A new statue of St. John the Baptist marked the church's 100th anniversary in 2014.

### 2016–2019
The Most Rev. Joseph J. Tyson, Bishop of Yakima, installed the Very Rev. Francisco Higuera as pastor of both parishes in July 2018; he celebrated his first Masses on July 21 and 22.
