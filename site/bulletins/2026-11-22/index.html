Reflection

Today we celebrate Christ the King, the last Sunday of the Church year. We might picture a king on a throne, far above ordinary people. In today's Gospel the King is found somewhere else.

When the Son of Man comes in glory, he does not ask how much we owned or how important we were. He asks whether we fed the hungry, welcomed the stranger, clothed the naked, and visited the sick and those in prison. Whatever we did for the least of his brothers and sisters, he says, we did for him.

Christ reigns from the Cross, and he waits for us in the people we meet each day. A shared meal, a visit, a kind word to someone lonely all serve the King. As the Church year ends and Thanksgiving comes, look for him in one person who needs you this week.

Who is one person in need through whom you can serve Christ the King this week?
