Reflection

Anyone who has spent a winter in the mountains knows the power can go out on the coldest night of the year. The people who get through it calmly checked the flashlight batteries in October.

In today's Gospel, ten bridesmaids wait for a bridegroom who runs late. All ten fall asleep. What separates them is not worry or energy but a little extra oil. Jesus asks us to keep something in reserve for the long wait.

Oil cannot be borrowed at midnight. Readiness is built slowly, in small ordinary habits. A quiet prayer in the morning. Mass when it would be easier to skip. A kindness done when no one is watching.

What small habit keeps your lamp filled this week?
