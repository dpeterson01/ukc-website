Praying with This Sunday's Gospel

On All Saints, the Church gives us the Beatitudes. Jesus goes up a mountain, sits down, and tells a crowd who is blessed: the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the meek, the merciful, the peacemakers. It is not a list of people who have it all together. It is a list of people who are leaning on God.

The first reading gives us a glimpse of where that leads: a great multitude from every nation, standing before the throne. Saints are not a special class far from us. They are neighbors who prayed, forgave, showed up, and kept trusting God through hard days, in places like Cle Elum and Roslyn as much as anywhere. Today we remember the saints we know by name and the many we do not. This week, think of one person whose faith helped shape yours. Thank God for them by name, and tell someone who knew them.

Whose faith has shaped mine, and how can I thank God for them this week?
