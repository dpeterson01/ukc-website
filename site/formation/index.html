Growing in faith

Faith formation

Faith is a journey, not a checkbox. We walk with people of every age, from children to lifelong Catholics to those exploring the faith for the first time.

Children · Oct–May
Religious education

Children's faith formation meets after the 10:00 a.m. Sunday Mass at St. John the Baptist, October through May. We form the next generation in the faith, from Baptism through First Communion and beyond. Sign up with the form below. Trish Griswold can answer anything the form does not.

Adults · Thursday evenings
OCIA, becoming Catholic

The Order of Christian Initiation for Adults is for anyone exploring the Catholic faith or completing their sacraments. We gather Thursday evenings at St. John the Baptist. Inquirers are always welcome, come as you are, at your own pace.
